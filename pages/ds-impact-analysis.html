Impact Analysis with Next Steps

Data Storytelling

Business Strategy

Core Business Problem

What is the impact of removing JavaScript from a gateway backend on risk identification and mitigation? Which business rules will be impacted? Will future credit applications have higher fraud risk?

Outcome

Improved stakeholder alignment and decision-making. Enabled more confident implementation planning. Accelerated agreement on next steps and strengthened the client’s trust in the recommended solution.

Context

A client’s integration engineer completed an initial impact analysis but needed support translating raw results into an executive-ready narrative. I reshaped the findings into a clear data story that highlighted the implications of proposed configuration changes and recommended next steps. The final presentation guided both the client and internal teams through recommended next steps for implementing a stronger solution. By reframing technical results into a business-focused storyline, stakeholders gained a clearer understanding of the path toward reduced risk.

Data Story Presentation

Approach

  • Reviewed the engineer's initial impact analysis to identify gaps in clarity, narrative structure, and executive-level interpretation.
  • Transformed raw technical findings into a clear data story that highlighted the business implications of proposed configuration changes.
  • Identified key business rules and the client's potential pain points associated with the proposed changes.
  • Compared gateway vs direct-integration scenarios to illustrate the potential impact and benefits of each approach.
  • Developed a structured narrative flow that connected analytical results to operational risk and recommended next steps.
  • Built an executive-ready presentation that guided both client and internal teams through configuration options and the rationale behind each recommendation.

Note: This analysis was developed collaboratively with clients and internal stakeholders.

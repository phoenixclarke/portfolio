Asset Utilization Executive Dashboard Suite

Dashboard Development

Business Strategy

Core Business Problem

Executive leadership had no visibility into which go-to-market (GTM) assets they owned and how content was being utilized. Which key performance indicators (KPIs) should be monitored to assess the utilization of GTM assets across different platforms? Which assets are underperforming or overperforming in terms of engagement and adoption?

Outcome

Content owners refined asset creation strategies based in data-driven logic. Executive leadership gained valuable operating insight into which products' assets provided the most value.

Context

As part of a modernization and server-migration effort, I designed operational and strategic dashboards to surface key performance metrics, consolidating end-user engagement signals from multiple hosting environments into a single view. These insights enabled content owners to understand reach, relevance, adoption, and usage; to refine asset creation strategies; and to better support product education in alignment with customer interest. This suite of asset-utilization dashboards gave executive leadership and asset owners a unified view of how go-to-market resources (demo videos, case studies,data-product library assets, and technical documentation) were being consumed across platforms.

Approach

  • Partnered with go-to-market content creators to identify asset types produced by several teams and obtained systems access to restricted data sources and enterprise accounts.
  • Implemented data governance practices to ensure data quality and consistency across all utilized platforms.
  • Developed a data model to consolidate asset utilization metrics across multiple platforms, including internal and external hosting environments, that included a taxonomy transparently tracing source systems, asset types, and content ownership.
  • Leveraged AI to quickly develop a Python script to extract data from external APIs with RESTful endpoints (e.g. Brightcove video analytics, Tableau Cloud usage metrics).
  • Built an Alteryx data pipeline that executed Python code, cleaned, standardized, and consolidated data across sources within a scheduled workflow for automated data refreshes.
  • Designed and implemented a suite of Tableau dashboards to surface the reach, relevance, adoption, and usage metrics in a single view.
  • Owned the project end-to-end, ensuring successful delivery, proactive communication of timelines, and training to boost adoption of the analytics solution.

Note: Data shown is synthetic and anonymized to protect proprietary information.

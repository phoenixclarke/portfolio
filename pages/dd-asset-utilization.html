Business Problem

As part of a modernization and server-migration effort, I designed operational and strategic dashboards to surface key performance metrics, consolidating end-user engagement signals from multiple hosting environments into a single view. These insights enabled content owners to understand reach, relevance, adoption, and usage; to refine asset creation strategies; and to better support product education in alignment with customer interest. This suite of asset-utilization dashboards gave executive leadership and asset owners a unified view of how go-to-market resources (demo videos, data-product library assets, and technical documentation) were being consumed across platforms.

Note: Data shown is synthetic and anonymized to protect proprietary information.

Business Strategy

Core Business Problem

A B2B SaaS launch of a new ML-based risk scoring product lacked strong value proposition to break into an established market. The analytical rigor needed to tie product output to monetary ROI uplift was missing.

Outcome

Pre-Sales conversations were strengthened with a standardized, visual storyline for communicating product value. Prospects gained a clearer understanding of the product's benefits and how it could drive measurable ROI.

Context

A B2B SaaS launch of a new ML-based risk scoring product stalled after prospects struggled to see value after proof-of-concept trials. Sales summaries lacked the analytical rigor needed to tie product output to monetary ROI uplift. I designed this Tableau data product as a pre-Sales framework to deliver a standardized, visual storyline for communicating product value – showcasing how Tableau can support both narrative delivery and real-time data exploration with live adjustments during discussions. This Tableau workbook served as both a presentation tool and interactive calculator, using animation and guided sequencing to keep audiences focused without overwhelming them with dense analysis.

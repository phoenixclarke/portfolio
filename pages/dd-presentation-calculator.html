Presentation Tool & Interactive Calculator

Dashboard Development

Business Strategy

Core Business Problem

Launch of a new ML-based risk scoring product lacked strong value proposition to break into an established market. What information is needed to communicate the value proposition effectively? How can we calculate monetary ROI uplift?

Outcome

Pre-Sales conversations were strengthened with a standardized, visual storyline for communicating product value. Prospects gained a clearer understanding of the product's benefits and how it could drive measurable ROI.

Context

A B2B SaaS launch of a new ML-based risk scoring product stalled after prospects struggled to see value after proof-of-concept trials. Sales summaries lacked the analytical rigor needed to tie product output to monetary ROI uplift. I designed this Tableau data product as a pre-Sales framework to deliver a standardized, visual storyline for communicating product value – showcasing how Tableau can support both narrative delivery and real-time data exploration with live adjustments during discussions. This Tableau workbook served as both a presentation tool and interactive calculator, using animation and guided sequencing to keep audiences focused without overwhelming them with dense analysis.

Approach

  • Partnered with Marketing team to identify the product differentiators currently included in pitch materials. Refined the language on how we are selling the product today (before the initiative).
  • Interviewed Sales, Customer Success, and Integration Engineering teams to understand the baseline data elements collected from clients to execute proof of concept (POC) analysis and generate standard product output for client retrospectives.
  • Mined the data to identify related variables, defensible proof points, key insights indicating product value, notable patterns and trends.
  • Prototyped an analytic solution that would summarize the POC output and would serve as a visualization tool without needing to build manual PowerPoint presentations and charts for each prospect's output.
  • Created a defensible monetary ROI model that estimated a prospect's return on investment based on automated decisions rooted in the product's risk score and the prospect's actual input costs and fraud losses.
  • Built a series of interactive Tableau dashboards that served as a single data story and ROI calculator that would be used to present the results of a proof of concept trial without creating any manual visualizations or fixed calculations.
  • Trained all client-facing teams, Product, and executive leadership on the new value proposition data story, how to use the new tool, and how to interpret the results effectively.

Personality Profile Explanation Tool

Dashboard Development

Business Strategy

Core Business Problem

DiSC Plus personality assessment results were complex and difficult to interpret without proper visualization. Individuals had no visibility into other family members' personality traits, and meaningful insights were buried in a 20-page qualitative report.

Outcome

Each family member gained a clearer understanding of their unique personality traits, how they interact with others, and how to improve their communication and interpersonal dynamics with others in the family.

Context

An interactive Tableau tool designed to explore and explain the results of my family's DiSC Plus personality Assessment, providing a structured way for each member to understand their natural and adaptive behaviors and motivators. The dashboard breaks personality insights into three levels – team dynamics, comparisons of individual values, and a detailed view of each person’s components and definitions – allowing users to navigate personality patterns in a clear, intuitive format. This personal project demonstrates how Tableau can be used to translate complex assessment data into an accessible, engaging, and meaningful visual experience for non-technical users.

Approach

  • Reviewed the DiSC Plus assessment results and identified key personality traits for each family member.
  • Highlighted measurable personality traits tied to each individual's behavior and motivators and influencing interpersonal dynamics.
  • Delivered a DiSC playbook structure that separated personality insights into three categories: team dynamics, individual comparisons, and detailed component analysis.
  • Built collaborative Tableau dashboards that allowed family members to explore and compare their personality profiles in an intuitive and engaging manner.
  • Supported visualizations with on-screen DiSC assessment data definitions that provided context and clarity on-hover and on-click.

Note: All original names and identifiers are anonymized to protect participant privacy.

Business Problem

An interactive Tableau tool designed to explore and explain the results of my family DiSC Plus personality Assessment, providing a structured way for each member to understand their natural and adaptive behaviors and motivators. The dashboard breaks personality insights into three levels – team dynamics, comparisons of individual values, and a detailed view of each person’s components and definitions – allowing users to navigate personality patterns in a clear, intuitive format. This personal project demonstrates how Tableau can be used to translate complex assessment data into an accessible, engaging, and meaningful visual experience for non-technical users.

Note: All original names and identifiers are anonymized to protect participant privacy.

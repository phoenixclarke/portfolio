Value Communication Training

Training

Business Strategy

Core Business Problem

Launch of a new ML-based risk scoring product lacked strong value proposition to break into an established market. What changes to the POC process are needed to communicate the value proposition effectively? What information should we request from prospects to start with a strong analyticalfoundation?

Outcome

Pre-Sales conversations were strengthened with a standardized, visual storyline for communicating product value. Prospects gained a clearer understanding of the product's benefits and how it could drive measurable ROI.

Context

A B2B SaaS launch of a new ML-based risk scoring product suite stalled after prospects struggled to see value after proof-of-concept trials. Qualitative summaries lacked the analytical rigor needed to demonstrate the product’s value. To address this gap, I redefined the end-to-end POC process with required inputs, standardized fields, and a clear interpretive framework that transformed trial results into quantified and monetary uplift. These training materials equipped Sales and Customer Success to collect the right evidence and communicate product value with a standardized storyline in go-to-market conversations.

Training Presentation

Approach

  • Partnered with Marketing team to identify the product differentiators currently included in pitch materials. Refined the language on how we are selling the product today (before the initiative).
  • Interviewed Sales, Customer Success, and Integration Engineering teams to understand the baseline data elements collected from clients to execute proof of concept (POC) analysis and generate standard product output for client retrospectives.
  • Redefined the end-to-end POC workflow by establishing required inputs, standardized fields, and consistent evidence-collection expectations for client-facing teams.
  • Built a clear interpretive framework that translated trial outputs into quantified and monetized uplift, shifting conversations from qualitative summaries to data-driven value stories.
  • Standardized the go-to-market storyline to ensure consistent messaging across teams.
  • Developed structured training materials that enabled client-facing teams to articulate value confidently, equipping them with repeatable narratives that resonated with business stakeholders and improved POC-to-sale conversion.

Note: This training program standardizes how teams communicate product value and ROI to stakeholders.

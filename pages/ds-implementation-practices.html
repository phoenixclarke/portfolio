Business Problem

Client support teams needed clearer guidance on newly released product functionality to help customers optimize fraud‑detection rules. To support the rollout, I created a structured training program that explained how the feature works, its adoption metrics, the universe of clients most likely to benefit, and the best practices identified through data mining and clustering. This resulting presentation ensured client-facing teams could confidently support the new release and guide customers toward high-impact configurations that maximize the value of the new release.

Resource Capacity & Workload Dashboard

Dashboard Development

Business Strategy

Core Business Problem

Modernization and migration of reporting platform would result in increased need for skilled analytics resources who could support client needs during the transition. Leadership needed a transparent, data‑driven way to understand the workload, capacity, costs, and the impact of using client-facing data scientists to support reporting.

Outcome

Business case to hire additional resources was approved. Revenue impact of offering data science services (rule optimization, recommendations, custom analysis, etc.) to clients was quantified and communicated effectively to stakeholders.

Context

Leadership needed a transparent, data‑driven way to understand the workload, capacity, costs, and the impact of upcoming data science initiatives. I developed a personal Tableau workbook to organize my team’s projects, to aggregate time-based costs and effort, and to forecast future resource needs – providing a clear operational view of analytics projects that ultimately supported the business case for expanding the team. I extracted and transformed underlying data from multiple project‑management systems into a unified and streamlined data structure, enabling consistent KPI reporting at both strategic and operational levels.

Resource Capacity & Workload Dashboard

Dashboard Development

Business Strategy

Core Business Problem

Reporting migration from legacy systems would result in all internal and external reports being unavailable for a period of time. What clients are most impacted? What is the impact of repurposing other data teams to support reporting during the migration? What are the costs and benefits of hiring additional resources during the migration? Is there sustainable workload to justify an FTE?

Outcome

Business case to hire additional resources was approved. Operational costs of repurposing data science services (rule optimization, recommendations, custom analysis, etc.) was quantified and communicated effectively to stakeholders.

Context

Modernization and migration of a reporting platform would result in increased need for skilled analytics resources who could support client reporting needs during the transition. Leadership needed a transparent, data‑driven way to understand the workload, capacity, costs, and impact of repurposing my client-facing data scientists to support reporting during the migration. I developed a personal Tableau workbook to organize my team’s projects, to aggregate time-based costs and effort, and to forecast future resource needs – providing a clear operational view of analytics projects handled by client-facing data scientists. This analysis ultimately supported the business case for expanding the team and became the foundation for future capacity planning.

Dashboard View

Approach

  • Extracted and transformed underlying data from multiple project-management systems into a unified and streamlined data structure.
  • Uploaded the data into a Snowflake data warehouse for further processing and analysis.
  • Used SQL to perform descriptive and diagnostic analysis of workload, to identify relevant KPI metrics, and to quantify year-over-year client analysis request trends impacting capacity.
  • Classified types of analysis requests to improve prioritization and understanding of client needs.
  • Built capacity forecast models that projected the strategic and operational needs.
  • Built a collection of Tableau dashboards that visualized future impact, costs by request type, and resource allocation.
  • Developed a business case to hire additional resources and presented needs assessment results to VP of Operations, the migration Product Manager, and Customer Success leadership.

Note: All employee and client names have been redacted to protect confidentiality.

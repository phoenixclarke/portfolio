Business Case

To identify pilot opportunities for a new payments platform within an existing global enterprise, I conducted a portfolio analysis across multiple lines of business (LOBs) to surface clients in target verticals with strong cross‑sell potential. The results were translated into a Tableau dashboard to visually explain the selection logic, to highlight the most promising opportunities, and to allow relationship managers to explore associated revenue and client details for better prioritization and research. This interactive view provided a structured, repeatable way to identify and evaluate cross‑sell candidates across the broader client ecosystem.

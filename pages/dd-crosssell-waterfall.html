Cross-Sell Opportunity Waterfall Analysis

Dashboard Development

Business Strategy

Core Business Problem

Release of a new payments platform within an existing global enterprise had slow adoption. Identify strong cross‑sell potential from other lines of business (LOBs). How should Sales and Relationship Management prioritize outreach

Outcome

Executive leadership obtained a clear understanding of potential cross-sell opportunities. Relationship managers could effectively prioritize and research these opportunities, leading to significant increase in platform usage and pilot adoptions.

Context

To identify pilot opportunities for a new payments platform within an existing global enterprise, I conducted a portfolio analysis across multiple lines of business (LOBs) to surface clients in target verticals with strong cross‑sell potential. The results were translated into a Tableau dashboard to visually explain the selection logic, to highlight the most promising opportunities, and to allow relationship managers to explore associated revenue and client details for better prioritization and research. This interactive view provided a structured, repeatable way to identify and evaluate cross‑sell candidates across the broader client ecosystem.

Approach

  • Owned the project end-to-end, ensuring successful delivery and training to executives and client relationship managers with low exposure to using any data tools (Excel and dashboards).
  • Partnered with Payment executives to understand the strategic objectives and identify the universe of clients with highest cross-sell potential: eCommerce-focused industries.
  • Conducted exploratory data analysis to uncover patterns in client attributes and behavior suggesting inclusion or exclusion from the target audience.
  • Used Excel advanced data analysis tools to create the client selection criteria, demonstrate the evaluation process, and document the decision-making framework.
  • Developed a scoring model to rank opportunities based on the overall client revenue (R12), fee-based revenue from existing payment card activity, and total shareholder value.
  • Designed and implemented an interactive Tableau dashboard that would clearly outline the selection process in a waterfall visualization outlining the reduction from total population to the addressable opportunity.
  • Interviewed relationship managers to understand their technical aptitude, their needs and preferences for researching client opportunities, and refined segmentation logic based on likelihood of engaging with the new payments platform.

Note: Data shown is synthetic and anonymized to protect proprietary information.

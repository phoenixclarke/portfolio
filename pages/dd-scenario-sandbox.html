Business Case

Clients attempting to optimize 50+ business rules targeting risky online behaviors were challenged by the product’s high complexity and quickly-evolving fraud techniques. I proposed a self-serve, near-real-time sandbox environment for clients to run scenario simulations, adjust risk rules, and test channel-specific configuration changes before deploying them in production. Using a Tableau-based prototype to demonstrate each business rule’s configuration options and the measurable impact, this prototype showcased how a future web application could streamline experimentation, reduce implementation risk, and give clients greater control over optimizing their SaaS configurations. This proposed web application established revenue-generating opportunities when sold alongside the product.

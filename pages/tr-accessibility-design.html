Accessibility & Inclusive Design

Training

Business Strategy

Core Business Problem

Dashboard users with visual disabilities were unable to interpret RAG (red, amber, green) status indicators, a popular design choice for displaying case status across enterprise reporting, limiting their ability to derive value from the data visualizations.

Outcome

Improved inclusivity and accessibility of dashboards for users with visual and motor disabilities, leading to a more usable data product. Raised design maturity of dashboard developers across business units.

Context

Internal dashboard users were experiencing accessibility challenges across the team’s dashboard library. I interviewed users, consolidated feedback, and built a practical, team-wide accessibility training program to introduce color-blind-safe design practices. These recommendations evolved into a set of best-practice design principles that raised the UI/UX maturity of junior and mid-level analysts. By standardizing accessible design expectations, the team produced dashboards that were more intuitive, inclusive, and aligned with modern data-visualization standards.

Training Presentation

Approach

  • Conducted user interviews and consolidated feedback to identify the specific accessibility barriers affecting dashboard usability across the organization.
  • Analyzed existing dashboard designs to assess their compliance with accessibility standards.
  • Developed a practical, team-wide training program focused on marketing-approved color-blind-safe palettes, contrast standards, and inclusive visual-design techniques.
  • Translated accessibility principles into actionable design guidelines, creating a repeatable framework junior and mid-level analysts could apply immediately.
  • Standardized dashboard design expectations to ensure consistency, reduce rework, and elevate the overall UI/UX maturity of the analytics team.
  • Trained team members and other dashboard developers using the same platform instance on implementing the new accessibility standards and design guidelines.
  • Provided an prioritized implementation plan and ongoing support system to address questions and reinforce learning.

Note: This training program was developed to enhance team capabilities in accessible design and inclusivity.

Fraud Product Value Framework

Data Storytelling

Business Strategy

Core Business Problem

Value proposition based solely on ML performance metrics were difficult for clients to understand and appreciate. Which value metrics show the clearest relationship between fraud detection accuracy and client outcomes? How can we improve retention and satisfaction?

Outcome

Enabled client-facing teams to deliver clearer, more compelling narratives, increasing alignment across functions and elevating the perceived value of the fraud-detection suite.

Context

Client conversations about a device-intelligence fraud solution were overly focused on machine-learning performance metrics, which limited clients' understanding of the product’s broader value. To expand the narrative, I constructed a robust and repeatable framework that connected fraud-catch categories, service reliability, and response-times with clear, monetized uplift that is client-strategy-focused. This approach helped Sales, Customer Success, and Product teams articulate the full value of the SaaS suite in terms that resonated with business stakeholders, moving discussions beyond basic false-positive and accuracy rates.

Data Story Presentation

Approach

  • Monitored and evaluated client conversations to identify where machine-learning metrics were limiting stakeholder understanding of broader product value.
  • Analyzed service reliability and response-time performance across clients to measure the real-world impact of fraud solution from an integration perspective.
  • Embedded monetization logic into the data story, enabling teams to articulate uplift in terms that resonate with executive decision-makers.
  • Developed a comprehensive framework to translate technical metrics into business outcomes.
  • Designed compelling data stories in PowerPoint with real client data that showcased the expanded narrative from model-centric to business-centric.
  • Equipped Sales, Customer Success, and Product teams with a standardized storyline and supporting visuals to communicate value consistently across engagements.

Note: This framework was used internally and with key clients to articulate comprehensive product value.

Business Problem

Client conversations about a device-intelligence fraud solution were overly focused on machine-learning performance metrics, which limited clients’ understanding of the product’s broader value. To expand the narrative, I delivered a repeatable framework that connected fraud-catch categories, service reliability, and response-times with clear, monetized uplift that is client-strategy-focused. This approach helped Sales, Customer Success, and Product teams articulate the full value of the SaaS suite in terms that resonated with business stakeholders, moving discussions beyond basic false-positive and accuracy rates.

Note: This framework was used internally and with key clients to articulate comprehensive product value.
